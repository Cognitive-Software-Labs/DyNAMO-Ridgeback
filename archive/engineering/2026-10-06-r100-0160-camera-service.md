# r100_0160 D455 camera service qualification

Recorded dates: 2026-10-06
Tested revisions: `7dad4dee6ec89e74f786f30aa75ffdbf3cc7b01e`, `f765d085c15edd0cc011e093fcd9a6369dd057b7`
Provenance: partial

The repository-installed camera service replaced the vendor camera stack on Intel
host `cpr-r100-0160` (namespace `r100_0160`, domain 0). The first install came
from `7dad4dee6ec89e74f786f30aa75ffdbf3cc7b01e`; the 1280×720 install, the
640×480 re-install before the reboot and the rollback-and-reinstall came from
`f765d085c15edd0cc011e093fcd9a6369dd057b7`. The two revisions differ only in the
installer's progress output and interrupt handling, so every run used the same
rendered driver parameters and unit. The driver was the apt
`ros-jazzy-realsense2-camera` 4.58.1 (librealsense 2.58.1) with D455 firmware
5.17.3.10 on USB 3.2; Clearpath packages were 2.9.12. The installed services DDS
profile restricted multicast to discovery (SHA-256
`a8c8a60cb1e339f032dc59ebd63a71b8849911cc3c233b64fd2dac7cd8e5cf1d`).

Contract-check reports remain on the robot under
`artifacts/hardware/20261006T*-camera-contract*/`. The installer snapshotted the
robot-local `robot.yaml` and vendor unit states before each first apply and
restored them on rollback; no copy is archived here. Vendor packages were not
pinned. These limits prevent exact reconstruction from this record alone.

## Method

The robot was stationary with motion disabled. After each install,
`tools/camera_contract_check --backend hardware` subscribed to colour, colour
CameraInfo and aligned depth, received images serialized and decoded only their
headers, and also received the front, rear and merged scans. It discarded ten
seconds of warm-up and measured sixty. Criteria were fixed before the runs:
message types; `rgb8`/`bgr8`/`rgba8`/`bgra8` colour and `16UC1`/`mono16`/`32FC1`
depth; one grid for colour, CameraInfo and depth equal to the installed profile;
one optical frame, `camera_0_color_optical_frame`; a `base_link` to optical TF
path at the last image stamp; at least 29 unique frames/s per image stream; no
duplicate or regressing stamps; no stamp gap above 150 ms; exact colour/depth
stamp pairing of at least 99 % both ways; no infrared or pointcloud topic under
`camera_0`; and every scan at least 38 Hz with no receive gap above 250 ms
(500 ms for the merger).

Before the first install, the Clearpath generators ran on a scratch copy of the
robot-local `robot.yaml` with the `camera_0` entry added. They accepted it,
generated no camera launch, and the expanded description ended at
`default_mount → camera_0_bottom_screw_frame` `[0.2692, 0, 0.725]` →
`camera_0_link` `[0.01115, 0.0475, 0.0145]`, the chain the simulators use.

## Results

All four runs passed every criterion.

| Run | Profile | Colour / depth unique rate | Largest stamp gap colour / depth | Pairing colour / depth | Scans (min rate; largest gap) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| First install | 640×480 | 29.97 / 29.97 Hz | 33.5 / 33.5 ms | 100.00 / 100.00 % | 39.92 Hz; 37.1 ms |
| Re-install | 1280×720 | 29.93 / 29.95 Hz | 66.7 / 66.7 ms | 100.00 / 99.94 % | 39.87 Hz; 50.2 ms |
| After cold reboot | 640×480 | 29.98 / 29.97 Hz | 33.5 / 66.8 ms | 99.94 / 100.00 % | 39.93 Hz; 53.9 ms |
| After rollback and reinstall | 640×480 | 29.95 / 29.93 Hz | 33.5 / 66.7 ms | 99.94 / 100.00 % | 39.92 Hz; 67.4 ms |

A 66.7 ms stamp gap is one missing frame. Single-frame losses occurred at both
profiles, so they are not a 1280×720 property. One `top` sample at 1280×720 put
the driver at 27.3 % of one core.

The host booted at 10:18:21 UTC and the service was active at 10:18:34 with no
restarts. The rollback restored the vendor units and `robot.yaml`; the reinstall
was run without `--serial` and enumerated the camera serial 338522302134. The
USB descriptor serial in sysfs is 346543063318 and does not select the camera.

## Operational findings

The first apply printed nothing while `clearpath-robot` restarted and was
interrupted after 56 seconds, leaving the vendor units disabled and the new
service unstarted; a rollback restored the snapshot. The installer has printed
each step and held interrupts during service changes since
`f765d085c15edd0cc011e093fcd9a6369dd057b7`.

Five of six `clearpath-robot` restarts took 60 seconds to stop
`clearpath-platform`; one took 7 seconds, and the stop during the reboot took
10 seconds. The cause was not investigated. A `proton_ros2_node` segmentation
fault in `librcl.so` was logged once during the first stop. Motor power and
teleop are unavailable during the stop.

At driver start the compressed-image transport plugin warned that `gop_size` was
unset; nothing subscribed to the compressed topics. During the last run,
discovery reported Unitree DDS topics (`rt/unitree_slam/high_rate_odometry`,
`rt/api/slam_operate/*`) on domain 0, so another robot shared the domain.

## Limits

These are stationary runs of sixty seconds each. They do not test the
application's detector or estimators (no perception environment was installed),
motion, sustained operation, camera–LiDAR calibration, or a comparison of the
factory calibration with the nominal simulation model.
