The two trajectory traces sit on top of each other — that is the point. Raw wheel odometry drifts by design (it is noise-injected), and the EKF pulls it back to within 2 cm of ground truth using the IMU. SLAM never sees ground truth; the walls it carved line up with the drive anyway.

What the robot saw

D455-native camera: 1280×720@~28 Hz, true 631 px focal length, rendered by RTX headless. Depth and a 921k-point cloud publish alongside (not shown).

One lidar scan, polar

Hokuyo UST-10LX, faithfully. 270° field of view with the rear 90° sector blank (the real sensor cannot see through its own mount), 0.25° steps, 0.06–10 m, range noise on. The idealized gz-era 360°/25 m/noise-free lidar is gone.
  • front + rear pair still covers 360° combined
  • slam_toolbox max_laser_range now 10 m to match
  • scan publishes ~20 Hz under co-tenant load — P5 gates RTF

What this proves

P0–P4 of the port are done: the robot imports from the same Clearpath description as the real platform, drives kinematically without fighting the floor, publishes the frozen topic contract over CycloneDDS, and the classic stack (robot_state_publisher, madgwick, robot_localization, slam_toolbox) runs against it unmodified.
